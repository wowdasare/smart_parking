What you can do

Everything for campus parking, in one place

From a standing permit down to a single afternoon's bay — with the gate checks that make both mean something.

Reserve a bay

Book a specific slot for a specific window and get a gate reference to show security on arrival.

Apply for a permit

Student, staff, visitor, or contractor cover — reviewed by the parking office, with the fee and validity tracked.

Real-time occupancy

See which zones and bays are available, occupied, or reserved before you leave the house.

Verified at the gate

Security checks a booking reference or a number plate and waves you through — or doesn't.

Stay notified

You'll get alerts on your dashboard and by email for booking confirmations and status changes.

Sensor-backed

Slot and gate sensors report occupancy over an authenticated API, with every change logged and auditable.

Questions

Common questions

They answer different questions. A permit is your standing right to park on campus for a term or a year. A booking holds one specific bay for a few hours. Security can check either at the gate.

Any time from now onwards. A single booking can run up to 24 hours, and you can cancel free of charge until the vehicle is checked in at the gate.

Two bookings can never overlap on the same bay — the system refuses the second one. If a bay is physically blocked anyway, security can move you and update the slot status on the spot.

Standard fees are GHS 50 for a student semester permit, GHS 120 for a staff academic year, GHS 10 for a short visitor stay, and GHS 200 for contractors. Fees are payable at the parking office once your application is approved.

Only you and the staff who need them. Your vehicles, bookings, and permits are scoped to your account; security sees the booking and permit status of a vehicle at the gate, and the parking office sees applications it has to decide on.
